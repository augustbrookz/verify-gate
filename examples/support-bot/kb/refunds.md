# Lumen Desk refund policy

Annual plans can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Add-on seats bought in the middle of a term are refunded only if they are removed within 7 days of purchase.
