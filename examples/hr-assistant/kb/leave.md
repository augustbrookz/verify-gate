# Northwind Atelier leave policy

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid vacation per month, up to 18 days per year. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for employees who have completed 6 months of service. Sick leave does not require a doctor's note for absences of up to 3 consecutive days.
